THE BEGINNING

A Love for Beauty, Elegance & Expression

Zahara Veloure by Kay was created from a love for beauty, femininity and timeless elegance.

We believe luxury is more than what you wear or the fragrance you choose. It is the feeling you carry with you — the quiet confidence, the presence and the way you express yourself.

Our collections are carefully selected to help every woman feel beautiful, confident and effortlessly refined.

From captivating fragrances to elegant abayas and fashion pieces, every detail is inspired by the idea that true luxury should feel personal.
